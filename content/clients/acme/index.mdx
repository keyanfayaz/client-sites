## 🏭 What We Build

### Precision Machined Components
Turned and milled parts in aluminum, stainless, and engineering plastics, held to ±0.005mm where the drawing calls for it.

### Fastening &amp; Hardware
A deep catalogue of standard and metric fasteners, plus custom runs when the standard part will not do.

### Maintenance &amp; Repair Supply
Bearings, seals, belts, and consumables stocked against your bill of materials so the line never waits on a five-dollar part.
