## 📉 Volume Breaks

Applied per line item, automatically, at time of order.

| Annual spend on the line | Discount off list |
| --- | --- |
| Up to $10,000 | List |
| $10,000 – $50,000 | 4% |
| $50,000 – $250,000 | 9% |
| $250,000 – $1,000,000 | 15% |
| Above $1,000,000 | Negotiated |

Breaks are calculated on trailing twelve-month spend and re-evaluated monthly. You do not need to commit up front to earn the rate.

## 🧾 What Is Never an Extra

Some suppliers meter these. We do not:

- Certificates of conformance and material test reports
- Dimensional inspection reports on machined batches
- API access, EDI transaction sets, and punchout catalogues
- Additional user seats and approval workflows
- Document retention for the full seven-year window

## ❓ Common Questions

**Is there a minimum order?**
No minimum on Standard. Managed Stock and Full Program accounts carry a monthly platform fee but no order minimum.

**How is freight handled?**
Freight is quoted at cost per shipment. Managed Stock and above include inbound freight on replenishment orders.

**Can we start on Standard and move up?**
That is the usual path. Most Managed Stock accounts spend two or three quarters on Standard first.

**What are the payment terms?**
Net 30 on approved credit. Net 45 and net 60 are available on annual contracts.
