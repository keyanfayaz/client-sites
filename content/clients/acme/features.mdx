## 📋 Ordering and Fulfilment

### Standing Orders
Set a reorder point per SKU. When stock at your facility drops below it, the replenishment order is raised automatically and held for your approval.

### Split Shipments
Take what is in stock now and let the balance follow, without raising a second purchase order.

### Will-Call and Dock Scheduling
Book a pickup window at any of the three warehouses. Your paperwork is printed and waiting.

## 🧾 Traceability and Compliance

Full material traceability is standard, not an upcharge:

| Document | Included | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Certificate of Conformance | Every order | PDF |
| Material Test Report | On request, per heat lot | PDF |
| Dimensional Inspection Report | Every machined batch | PDF / CSV |
| RoHS and REACH Declarations | Every applicable SKU | PDF |

Documents attach to the order record and stay retrievable for seven years.

## 🔌 Integrations

Acme talks to the systems you already run:

- **ERP** — SAP, NetSuite, and Epicor connectors with two-way order sync
- **Punchout** — cXML and OCI punchout catalogues for procurement portals
- **EDI** — 850, 855, 856, and 810 transaction sets
- **API** — REST endpoints for pricing, availability, and order placement

## 🔐 Account Controls

- Role-based approval chains with configurable spend thresholds
- Cost-centre tagging on every line item for clean chargebacks
- Per-user catalogue restrictions so buyers only see approved parts
- Single sign-on via SAML or OIDC
