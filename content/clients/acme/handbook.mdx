# Handbook

The rules of the road. Where a standard is externally audited, that is called out — those are not negotiable at local level.

---

## 📏 Quality Policy

Acme holds ISO 9001 and AS9100 certification. Both depend on us doing what we say we do, every time, and being able to prove it.

<div class="bg-white p-6 rounded-lg shadow-md border-l-4 border-blue-600 my-6">
  <p class="font-semibold mb-2">The four commitments</p>
  <ol class="text-gray-700 space-y-2 text-sm">
    <li><strong>Build to the drawing.</strong> If the drawing is wrong or ambiguous, stop and raise it. Do not interpret.</li>
    <li><strong>Record what you did.</strong> An unrecorded inspection did not happen as far as an auditor is concerned.</li>
    <li><strong>Escalate nonconformance immediately.</strong> Concealing a defect is the one thing that ends employment here.</li>
    <li><strong>Traceability travels with the part.</strong> Heat lot, operator, and inspection stay attached from raw stock to dispatch.</li>
  </ol>
</div>

**Externally audited.** Surveillance audits run annually; findings are published internally within ten working days.

## 🦺 Health and Safety

- Stop-work authority belongs to **everyone**, at any level, without needing permission
- Near misses are reported even when nothing was damaged — they are the cheapest data we get
- PPE requirements are posted at every zone entrance and apply to visitors identically
- Lockout and tagout is required for any intervention past a guard, no matter how brief

Safety performance is reviewed at the monthly operations meeting and the numbers are posted on the floor, good or bad.

## 💳 Travel and Expense

**What is covered**

- Economy airfare and standard rail; business class only above six hours, with prior approval
- Accommodation up to the city cap published in the resources index
- Meals at actual cost with a receipt, up to the daily cap
- Mileage at the published rate for use of a personal vehicle

**What is not**

- Alcohol, in-room entertainment, and fines of any kind
- Upgrades paid personally then claimed back
- Anything without a receipt above the minor-expense threshold

Claims go in within thirty days. Approval sits with your manager up to the delegated limit, and with Finance above it.

## 🤝 How We Treat Each Other

We are a manufacturer with a long-tenured workforce, and most people here will work together for years. That only holds if the basics hold.

- Disagree in the meeting, not afterwards in the corridor
- Criticise the decision, never the person who made it
- Bad news travels immediately and upward — nobody is punished for being the messenger
- Harassment or discrimination results in investigation and, where founded, dismissal

Concerns can be raised with any manager, People Ops, or the confidential reporting line listed in the resources index. Reports made in good faith are protected, whatever the outcome of the investigation.
