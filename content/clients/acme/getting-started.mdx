# Getting Started

Everything a new starter needs in their first five days. Work through it in order — a few items gate the others.

---

## ⚠️ Before You Set Foot on the Floor

<div class="bg-amber-50 border-l-4 border-amber-500 p-5 rounded-r-lg my-6">
  <p class="font-semibold text-amber-900 mb-1">Safety induction is mandatory and blocking</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-amber-800">You cannot enter production areas — including for a tour — until induction is complete and recorded. There are no exceptions, including for visitors and executives.</p>
</div>

The induction covers:

1. Emergency stops, muster points, and alarm tones
2. Personal protective equipment by zone
3. Lockout and tagout basics
4. Reporting a near miss
5. Where the first-aid stations and eyewash points are

Book through People Ops. It runs Monday and Wednesday mornings and takes about two hours.

## 🔑 Systems Access

Access is provisioned by role, not by request. Your manager selects your role profile and the systems below are granted together on day two.

| System | What it is for | Role profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| This knowledge base | Standards, playbooks, training | Everyone |
| ERP | Orders, inventory, purchasing | Everyone |
| Quality system | Inspection records, nonconformances | Production, Quality |
| Shop scheduler | Job routing and machine load | Production, Planning |
| Applications portal | Drawings, tolerance queries | Engineering, Sales |

Sign-in is single sign-on across all of them. If a system asks you to create a separate account, that is a mistake — stop and raise it with IT rather than working around it.

Need something outside your profile? Your manager raises an access request; approval sits with the system owner, not with IT.

---

## 🙋 Who To Ask

| Situation | Go to | How fast |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Something is unsafe right now | Nearest supervisor, then EHS | Immediately, stop work |
| A part looks wrong | Quality, before it moves | Same shift |
| A machine is down | Maintenance via the line-down process | Immediately |
| A customer is unhappy | Your account engineer | Same day |
| Anything about pay or leave | People Ops | Within two days |
| You do not know who to ask | Your buddy, then your manager | Any time |

Asking early is always cheaper than guessing. Nobody here has ever been criticised for stopping to check.

---

## ✅ End of Week One

You should have completed safety induction, gained systems access, walked the floor, started your learning path, and agreed thirty-day goals with your manager. If any of those are missing, tell People Ops — it is a process failure, not yours.
