## 📈 A Representative Engagement

A transit authority came to us with 340 obsolete part numbers across an aging fleet and no reliable source for any of them.

**What we did**

1. Audited the bill of materials and identified 210 parts still available through standard channels
2. Reverse engineered 94 components from surviving samples and original drawings
3. Placed the remaining 36 on a last-time-buy with managed storage
