# About Acme Corp

Acme has been making and moving industrial components since 1984. We are still privately held, still run by people who came up through the shop, and still judged on whether the right part arrives on the right day.

## 🎯 What We Actually Believe

**The part is not the product. The delivery date is.**
A perfect component that arrives Thursday when the line stopped Tuesday has no value. We optimise for the date first.

**Paperwork is part of the part.**
If you cannot prove what an item is made of, you cannot install it in a regulated environment. Certification travels with every order.

**Answer the phone.**
Applications engineers sit on the same floor as the schedulers. When something goes wrong, the person who picks up can actually fix it.
