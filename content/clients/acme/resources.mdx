## Glossary

**AS9100** — Aerospace quality management standard. Builds on ISO 9001 with additional traceability and risk requirements.

**First article** — The first part off a new setup, inspected in full against the drawing before the run proceeds.

**Gauge R and R** — A study establishing how much of the observed measurement variation comes from the gauge and operator rather than the parts.

**Heat lot** — A batch of material from a single melt, tracked so any part can be traced to its raw stock.

**Line-down** — Production stopped with no restart in sight. Triggers the escalation playbook.

**NCR** — Nonconformance report. Raised whenever a part does not meet the drawing.

**PM** — Preventive maintenance, performed on a schedule rather than in response to failure.

**Router** — The document travelling with a job listing every operation in sequence, with sign-offs.

**Stop-work authority** — Anyone's right to halt work on safety or quality grounds without seeking permission first.
