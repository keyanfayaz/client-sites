## 📝 Request a Quote

Send the following to **quotes@acme.example** and we will respond within one business day:

1. Part number or drawing (PDF, STEP, or DXF)
2. Quantity and required delivery date
3. Material and finish, if the drawing does not specify
4. Any certification you need attached

If you are quoting a whole assembly, attach the bill of materials as CSV and we will price it line by line.
