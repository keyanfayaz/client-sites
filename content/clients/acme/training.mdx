## Certification matrix

Which tickets each role must hold and how often they are renewed.

| Certification | Production | Quality | Maintenance | Office | Renewal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Safety induction | Required | Required | Required | Required | Every 2 years |
| Lockout and tagout | Required | — | Required | — | Annual |
| Forklift operation | By area | — | Required | — | Every 3 years |
| First aid | 1 per shift | — | 1 per shift | 1 per floor | Every 3 years |
| Precision measurement | By role | Required | — | — | Every 2 years |
| Internal audit | — | Required | — | — | Every 2 years |

Expiring certifications surface in your manager's dashboard sixty days out. A lapsed certification means you come off that task until it is renewed — no discretion.

---

## Development beyond the matrix

The matrix is the floor, not the ceiling.

- **Apprenticeships.** Four-year machining apprenticeship with day release. Two intakes a year.
- **External study.** Tuition support for job-relevant qualifications, agreed in your development plan.
- **Cross-training.** Rotations into an adjacent function for a defined period. Most of our supervisors came through this route.
- **Conference and trade attendance.** One event a year per person, budgeted at team level.

Ask in your development conversation rather than waiting to be offered — the budget exists and is routinely underspent.

## How sign-off works

1. Complete the modules in the path
2. Demonstrate the skill to an assessor, on real work, not a mock-up
3. The assessor records the sign-off against your record with the date
4. Renewal is scheduled automatically from that date

Assessors are certified themselves and cannot sign off someone they directly manage — that separation is audited.
